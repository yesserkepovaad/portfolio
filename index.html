Welcome

Alua Yesserkepova

History Graduate Student  ·  Researcher  ·  Analyst

I am a history graduate student specializing in postcolonial and Eurasian studies, with a focus on Soviet legacies, Francoist Spain, and comparative historical analysis. My research combines archival methodology with multilingual and cross-cultural competencies, bringing historical rigor to complex contemporary questions.

Research Focus

Academic Interests

🌍

Postcolonial Studies

Examining colonial legacies and their lasting impact on societies, institutions, and identities — with particular focus on Central Asia and the post-Soviet space.

☭

Soviet Union

Researching Soviet-era policies, governance, and ideology, with attention to how the Soviet legacy continues to shape successor states and regional identities today.

🇪🇸

Spain under Franco

Analysing Francoist authoritarianism, cultural repression, and the politics of historical memory in 20th-century Spain — with direct engagement with Spanish-language sources.

⚖️

Comparative Analysis

Applying cross-national and cross-temporal frameworks to identify patterns of political change, authoritarian governance, and post-colonial transition across different regions.

🗺️

Eurasian Studies

Exploring the intersection of European and Asian historical processes across the Eurasian continent, with a focus on sovereignty, regional identity, and geopolitical change.

🇪🇸

Minor in Spanish & Hispanic Studies

Formal academic training in Spanish language, Latin American and Iberian history, and Hispanic cultural analysis. Enables direct engagement with Spanish-language primary sources, archival materials, and regional scholarship — a professional asset for organizations operating in or researching Spanish-speaking markets and contexts.
